Delete your Storm Engine data

Storm Engine by Moonbit Studios

You can delete the data the Storm Engine game servers keep about you at any time: straight from the game, or by email if you no longer have the game installed. Storm Engine has no username or password to remember.

Option 1: in the game (instant)

  1. Open Storm Engine.
  2. Tap the menu button at the top right, then Settings (or Settings on the home screen).
  3. Under Privacy, tap Delete my online data and confirm.

Your online data is deleted right away and online features are switched off.

Option 2: by email

Email studiosmoonbit@gmail.com with the subject Delete my Storm Engine data and your player name as shown in the game (for example Storm_Chaser#1234; it appears on the home screen under the title). We delete your data within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What is deleted

  • Your anonymous player account (player ID)
  • Your cloud save (game progress stored online)
  • Your published island layout
  • Your player name and your leaderboard and tournament scores. If an entry remains in an archived weekly ranking, email us and we remove it.

What is kept

  • Game progress saved on your phone stays there until you uninstall the game or use Settings > Save > Reset progress.
  • Purchase records are kept by Google Play under Google's policies. We never store payment details.
  • Ad partners (Unity LevelPlay and Unity Ads) keep ad data under their own policies. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings.

We keep no copy of deleted data. More details: Privacy Policy.
