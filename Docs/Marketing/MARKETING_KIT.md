# Storm Engine marketing kit

Everything here is ready to copy. Store art lives in `Tools/marketing/store/`, the website in `Docs/Website/`.

## The pitch
- **Name:** Storm Engine · **Store title:** Storm Engine: Idle Storms · **Slogan:** Build smart. Storm big.
- **One line:** A cozy idle game where you build a weather machine and unleash giant storms.
- **Elevator pitch:** Build pumps, coils, furnaces and turbines that multiply into Storm Power, unleash storms that
  chain lightning into huge combos, discover phenomena like Cyclones and Supercells, decorate a floating island,
  and compete in a weekly tournament where every score is replayed and checked by the server.
- **Why it stands out:** a real 3D diorama instead of flat menus; storms you *watch* happen; fair, server-verified
  leaderboards; no forced ads (only rewarded ones the player chooses); 12 languages from day one.
- **Audience:** idle and incremental fans, cozy-game players, optimizers who love multiplier math (16-35).

## Store search (ASO)
- Title and short description already carry the main words: *idle, storms, weather machine, leaderboards*.
- Words to keep in the full description: idle, incremental, weather, storm, lightning, tycoon-style upgrades,
  island, decorate, tournament, offline progress, cozy.
- Do not stuff keywords or name other games: Google penalizes both.
- After launch, compare conversion per language in **Play Console > Grow > Store performance** and try one store
  listing experiment at a time (for example icon A/B test).

## Closed test: finding 12+ testers (needed before production)
Where to ask: friends and family with Android phones; Reddit **r/AndroidClosedTesting** and **r/TestersCommunity**
(tester swaps); Discord servers for "12 testers" exchanges; your social accounts. Testers must keep the app
installed and stay opted in for 14 days, so ask 20 people to be safe.

Message to copy:
> Hi! I made a cozy Android idle game called **Storm Engine**: you build a weather machine and unleash giant storms.
> Google needs 12 testers for 14 days before I can release it. Could you join? 1) Join with your Gmail: [opt-in link]
> 2) Install from Google Play 3) Play a little and keep it installed for two weeks. Any feedback is gold. Thank you! ⚡

## Short videos (TikTok, YouTube Shorts, Reels)
Record on the phone (Android screen recorder), portrait, 15-30 seconds, no menus in the first two seconds.
1. **Hook (0-2 s):** the storm button pressed, lightning pillar hits the dome. Text: "I built a machine that makes storms".
2. **Build (2-8 s):** buying machines fast, numbers climbing, a new machine appearing on the island.
3. **Payoff (8-18 s):** all four boosts fired during a storm, "PERFECT WINDOW", the score exploding.
4. **Island (18-24 s):** slow pan over a decorated island.
5. **End card (24-30 s):** icon + "Storm Engine · Free on Google Play".
Post 2-3 per week around launch; the ones that get views tell you what to show next.

Play Store promo video: upload the best 30-second cut to YouTube (unlisted is fine) and add the link in the store
listing.

## Already built into the game
- **Share** button on record storms and in Settings (opens the phone's share sheet with the store link).
- **Rating card** from Google Play after level-ups (Engine Level 7, then 20); Play limits how often it appears.
- **Retention:** daily gift and streak, three daily missions, weekly tournament with rewards, reminders.
- **Social:** leaderboards with player names, visiting other players' islands.

## Numbers to watch (Play Console)
- Store listing conversion (visitors → installs) per country and language.
- Day-1 / day-7 retention (Statistics), uninstalls in the first day.
- Android vitals: crashes and ANRs; ratings and review themes.
- Ad revenue per daily user (LevelPlay dashboard) and how many players watch at least one ad.
